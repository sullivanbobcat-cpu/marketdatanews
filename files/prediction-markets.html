Market Data Infrastructure Implications

Feed Infrastructure
Exchange-Grade Data Requirements Emerging
Prediction markets generate real-time binary contract price data with increasing velocity. As platforms scale to billions in daily volume — Polymarket hit $425M in a single day on February 28, 2026 — feed infrastructure requirements are approaching those of traditional exchanges. Firms building on these platforms need low-latency data feeds, historical data archives, normalized order book data, and surveillance capabilities equivalent to regulated equities venues.
Market Data Costs
Free API Access Won't Last
Unlike traditional exchanges, Kalshi and Polymarket currently offer free REST and WebSocket API access to real-time and historical data. This pricing model will not persist as platforms mature, seek revenue diversification, and face the infrastructure costs of serving institutional clients. Market data professionals should document current data consumption patterns now — before fee schedules are introduced. Early API integrations may be grandfathered; late adopters will pay institutional rates.
Compliance Implications
DCM Obligations Apply in Full
CFTC-registered DCMs are required to maintain complete audit trails, conduct ongoing market surveillance, and enforce rules against manipulation and insider trading. Firms trading on prediction markets face the same reporting obligations as traditional derivatives markets, including position reporting and large trader notification thresholds. Under proposed CFTC rules, CAT reporting requirements may extend to event contracts. Compliance teams should treat prediction market activity as equivalent to futures trading for internal policy purposes.
