23/5 Extended Trading — Market Data Implications

Connectivity & Feed Handler Impact

NYSE Arca now opens at 2:30am ET for its pre-opening session and begins executing trades at 4:00am ET in the early trading session. This means market data feeds begin disseminating quote and trade data significantly earlier than the traditional 9:30am open.

Connectivity teams should ensure feed handlers are active and monitored from 2:30am ET. Systems that assume a 9:30am start will miss early session data and may produce incorrect open prices, VWAP calculations, and intraday risk metrics.

SIP feed implications: CTA and UTP SIP feeds begin processing data from the early session. Firms subscribed to consolidated tape receive early session quotes and trades from 4:00am ET. Consolidated last-sale data is available before the traditional 9:30am core session open.

Overnight risk management: Firms with overnight risk systems need to account for pre-4am price discovery. Opening prices on NYSE Arca-listed securities may be established in the early session, affecting mark-to-market calculations and risk limits before the core session opens.
