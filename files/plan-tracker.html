~Aug 2026		 Expansion of OPRA Data Dissemination: 48-Line → 96-Line Multicast Network
OPRA is doubling its multicast line capacity from 48 to 96 lines to accommodate continued options market volume growth. Subscribers must provision additional network connections and update feed handler configurations. This is the single most impactful OPRA infrastructure change in recent years. Capacity planning and procurement should begin immediately. 		Infrastructure Active — Plan Now OPRA-C40912
Mar 27, 2026 48-Line Expansion Notice — Updated Subscriber Guidance Published
OPRA published updated technical specifications for the 96-line expansion. Connectivity providers and co-location customers should review bandwidth and switch port requirements. Previous 48-line notice (C40164) is withdrawn and superseded. 		Milestone Superseded OPRA-C40164
TBD 2026 OPRA Fee Schedule Review — Annual Update
OPRA Operating Committee conducting annual fee schedule review. Changes to subscriber and vendor fees expected to be announced Q2 2026 with an effective date of Q3/Q4 2026. Watch for SEC filing. 		Fee Change Pending OPRA-FEE-2026
Why the 96-line expansion matters: OPRA currently processes over 100 billion messages per day — more volume than all equity markets combined. The move from 48 to 96 multicast lines is a direct response to the continued growth in options market activity. Firms that fail to provision the additional connections before the production cutover will experience data loss. This is not optional. Connectivity teams should begin infrastructure planning immediately regardless of the official go-live date.
