The Intelligence Layer for Market Data Infrastructure

Independent coverage of exchange connectivity, NMS plans, regulatory deadlines, and market data economics — built for the professionals who keep markets running.

Exchange notices get buried in PDFs. Regulatory deadlines slip through the cracks. Fee schedules change without warning. A 1Gbps connection quietly stops supporting multicast and someone's feed goes dark at 9:31am.

Market data infrastructure is the plumbing of financial markets. When it works, nobody notices. When it fails, trading halts, compliance teams scramble, and operations managers spend their weekends on calls with exchange support.

Market Data News exists to surface the changes that matter before they become problems.
