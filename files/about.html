About Market Data News
Mission · Philosophy · FeedWatch · Contact
Market Data News exists because the plumbing of financial markets — the feeds, the protocols, the deadlines, the migrations — matters enormously to the people who build and operate them, and no one was covering it the way it deserved to be covered.
Our Mission

Intelligence for the people who keep markets running

Exchange notices get buried in PDFs. Regulatory deadlines slip through the cracks. A 1Gbps connection quietly stops supporting multicast and someone's feed goes dark at 9:31am. Market Data News was built to prevent that — to surface the technical and regulatory changes that matter to data engineers, operations teams, compliance professionals, and infrastructure architects before they become problems.

We cover the full stack: exchange protocol updates, NMS plan amendments, regulatory deadlines, feed changes, port migrations, and the broader market structure shifts that shape how data moves. Our FeedWatch calendar is the operational backbone — a living reference for every deadline and action item affecting the industry.

We are editorially independent. We don't take direction from exchanges, data vendors, or financial sponsors. Our only obligation is to the professionals who rely on this information to do their jobs.

How It Works

Inside FeedWatch

FeedWatch is our flagship product — a continuously updated calendar of exchange notices, regulatory deadlines, feed changes, and infrastructure migrations that require action from market data professionals.

FeedWatch
Market Infrastructure Intelligence · How we build it
1
Source MonitoringWe monitor primary sources directly — CME Globex notices, NYSE and NASDAQ SEC rule filings, FINRA regulatory notices, SEC Federal Register postings, CFTC advisories, and NMS Plan amendments — as they are published.
2
Triage & ClassificationEach item is reviewed, classified by impact level (Critical / High / Medium / Low), tagged by organization and category, and assessed for whether it requires direct action from firms.

Operational SummariesEvery FeedWatch entry is written as an operational brief — what changed, what the deadline is, what firms need to do, and a direct link to the source notice. No fluff.
4
Ongoing MaintenanceEntries are updated as situations evolve — when deadlines shift, when firms receive extensions, or when follow-on notices are published. The calendar reflects current reality, not just the original announcement.

Editorial Standards

How we approach coverage

Every piece of information on this site traces back to a primary source — an official exchange notice, a regulatory filing, an SEC order. We link to the source on every FeedWatch entry. We don't publish rumors or unverified information.

When we characterize the impact of a change as Critical or High, that assessment reflects the operational consequence for firms running market data infrastructure — not a marketing judgment. We err on the side of surfacing things that matter and letting professionals decide what to act on.

We have no sponsored content, no paid placements, and no arrangements that could influence our coverage. If that changes, we'll say so clearly.
