Recent Enforcement Actions

FINRA
Broker-Dealer Fined $2.1M for CAT Reporting Failures
FINRA fined a registered broker-dealer $2.1 million for systematic failures in Consolidated Audit Trail reporting obligations. Deficiencies included late submissions, missing order events, and inadequate supervisory controls over CAT reporting infrastructure.
March 2026
SEC
SEC Issues Guidance on AI Use in Market Data Dissemination
The SEC's Division of Trading and Markets issued staff guidance addressing the use of artificial intelligence in market data feed processing, dissemination, and real-time analytics. Guidance clarifies existing obligations under Reg NMS and Exchange Act Rule 17a-4 as applied to AI-generated data products.
February 2026
CFTC
Swap Data Repository Fined $800K for Reporting Failures
The CFTC assessed an $800,000 civil monetary penalty against a registered swap data repository for failures to accurately report swap transaction data under Part 49 reporting requirements. Errors included missing counterparty identifiers and delayed trade confirmation submissions.
January 2026
SEC
Firm Charged with Rule 605 Non-Compliance for Stale Execution Data
The SEC charged a registered market center with violations of Rule 605 of Regulation NMS for publishing execution quality reports containing materially stale data. The firm's reporting infrastructure failed to incorporate intraday execution updates, resulting in reports that did not accurately reflect actual execution quality metrics.
December 2025
